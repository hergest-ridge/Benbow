# UX

## Principles
1. **Instant.** Everything opens from the local cache. Writes are optimistic. No spinners for local data.
2. **Smooth.** 120 Hz, zero jank in feeds, shared-element transitions, predictive back, spring motion, subtle haptics. Telegram is the bar.
3. **Seamless text.** It reads and edits like a notebook, not a block database (see editor.md).
4. **Low friction.** Capture in under 3 seconds. Metadata is optional and can always be added later.
5. **Forgiving.** Undo everywhere, trash instead of delete, version history, input that never gets lost.
6. **Calm.** Warm, paper-like, unhurried. No guilt mechanics, no noisy badges.

## Navigation (phone)
Bottom bar: **Главная · Лента · Календарь · Поиск · Ещё**. The quick-capture composer lives on Home only. "Ещё" (Mihon-style "More") holds the rest: server and sync, reminders, tags, trash, later settings.
Motion (owner's feedback, 2026-10-13): tabs switch in place, no animation. A screen opened on top fades in over the one below (220 ms); Back and the predictive back gesture fade it away (no scale-down). The tab bar is the compact M3 Expressive `ShortNavigationBar`; it stays in place while the keyboard is up (the keyboard covers it and slides off it), and screens above get the keyboard inset minus the bar.
Desktop and web: navigation rail + adaptive list-detail panes (Material 3 adaptive), with keyboard shortcuts.
As built (6.6): from 840 dp wide (desktop, tablets) a navigation rail replaces the bottom bar and screens are centred at up to 820 dp; the layout tree keeps its shape, so resizing never rebuilds screens. Desktop: closing the window hides it to the tray (reminders and sync keep running; a one-time notification says so), tray menu Открыть / Новая запись / Выход, window size and place remembered (a place on a missing monitor falls back), files dragged from Explorer onto an entry are attached, shortcuts Ctrl+N, Ctrl+F, Ctrl+1…5, Ctrl+S (save a version), Alt+← (listed under "Ещё → Сочетания клавиш"). The native Windows title bar is painted in the app's background and text colors through DWM (JNA 5.19.1; caption color on Windows 11, dark caption on Windows 10), so it blends in while snap layouts, shadow, rounded corners and edge resizing stay native. As built (W4, 2026-10-16, desktop and web): from 1040 dp wide the tab's screen stays on the left (400 dp) and what it opens shows on the right (centred at up to 820 dp): a post, an entry, comments, a page from "Ещё"; the entry open on the right is framed in the list. Picking another item replaces the right side instead of stacking; Back (or the post's back arrow) closes it, and an empty right side shows the wordmark, a hint and "Новая запись". The rail stays beside an open entry; the media viewer and the drawing canvas take the whole window. Web: the address follows the screen (`#/feed`, `#/entry/<id>`, `#/server`; a reload, a bookmark or a typed address opens it), and the browser's Back, Alt+← and a mouse's side button go back in the app; at its first screen they leave the page. Web keys: Alt+N new entry and Alt+1…5 tabs (the browser keeps Ctrl+N and Ctrl+1…5), Ctrl+F search, Ctrl+S save a version; "Ещё → Сочетания клавиш" lists them. A loading screen (the lantern icon and a thin bar) covers the first seconds and fades out; the tab icon is the app icon; a failed start says so there. As built (W5): "Ещё → Экспорт" downloads the ZIP; "Импорт из файла…" in the calendars panel takes Google's ZIP or an `.ics`; reminders ring while the tab is open, as a system notification once the browser may show them (asked when a reminder is added), otherwise as a message in the app. As built (owner's web feedback, 2026-10-16, server 0.7.2): the line between the list and the right side drags (the list 300 to 640 dp, the right side keeps at least 480; remembered per device, a double click puts it back to 400); with a mouse (desktop and web) every carousel and the viewer get round translucent ‹ › buttons at the sides, hidden at either end (touch screens keep swiping only); in the viewer ← and → page and Esc closes; whatever can be clicked shows the pointing hand; a carousel is at most 70% of the window tall (phones never reach it), and pictures in the text stay at most 520 dp tall as before.

- **Editor:** full-screen, distraction-free. Toolbar above the keyboard (format, checklist, attach, voice, template, `/`). Backgrounds Keep-style. Metadata sheet (day, tags, mood, kind). History and conflicts accessible from the menu.
- **Dictation** (owner 2026-10-17, ADR-033; 7.10b-e, Android only): two keys like a mechanical dictaphone's, side by side in one housing at the right end of the editor's media row, engraved «голос» and «текст» («текст» nearer the thumb). At rest a key stands a little raised; pressed it travels down fast with a firm click at the bottom (ToggleOn); a tap leaves it latched down with its lamp lit (red for голос, amber like the lantern for текст), the next tap lets it spring up with a small bounce and a soft click (ToggleOff) and keeps the take; holding records only while held. Pressing one key while the other is down lets that one up, its take kept (a tape deck's interlock). The lamp and a level bar follow the voice. While a key is down, the row's left part shows "Текст 0:12" or "Голос 0:12", the level and ✕ (голос: the take is thrown away; текст: this take's text comes out again, ↶ brings it back). Text arrives phrase by phrase at the cursor (after the text when it isn't focused) as typed text: one undo step per phrase, the cursor's marks (inside a spoiler it stays a spoiler); phrases still being recognized show as three pulsing dots in the strip (not in the text, which holds only real words). No sound: it would get into the take. Without the model the текст key is locked: half a press, a spring back (Reject) and "Для текста голосом нужна модель · Открыть". When recognition fails the take becomes a voice note and the screen says so; after a good take its audio is deleted, "Текст вставлен · Приложить запись" offers it for a few seconds. Quick notes (Home, the «Заметка» sheet, comments, the reply under a post) get the текст key in the same housing beside the field, without a strip (the lamp shows it listening; a second tap ends it); the «Заметка» sheet says under its field what a snackbar says elsewhere. A take that has to stay a voice note goes to the editor's entry, or from a quick note to a new note of its own. In the editor a voice note with its transcript shows «Заменить текстом» at the end of its row: the words take its place in the text (↶ undoes), the voice note leaves the entry, and "Голосовое заменено текстом · Вернуть" brings it back (into the text too while the editor is open).
- **Sounds and the player** (owner 2026-10-17, ADR-035; 7.12): any sound file joins an entry (Android: «Аудио» in the editor's media row opens the system's file picker; desktop and web: the file dialog, drops, pastes; sharing to Benbow). A sound shows like a voice note: a row with the cover (the play button over it, a little dimmed) or the round button, the title (the caption given by hand, else the file's tag, else its name), the artist and the length; the current track's row shows the seek bar and the time. Rows never fetch the audio: a tap does, with a spinner in the button. The feed lists every sound of a post under its text, wherever it sits in the document. One player for all sounds, voice notes too. A tap in the feed queues that post's sounds and then those of the posts below it (older), skipping posts under a curtain; elsewhere the entry's own sounds. The mini-player is a 56 dp strip above the tab bar (on a wide window at the foot of the content column; not on pushed phone screens): play/pause, the title over the artist (a voice note: when it was recorded), «Следующий трек» while playing and ✕ «Закрыть плеер» while paused, a hairline of progress along its bottom; it folds in and out. A tap or a swipe up opens the player: on a phone a sheet as tall as the screen (a swipe down or Back closes it), on a wide window a dialog. It takes its colors from the cover (the cover's main color as the seed of the app's scheme, a gradient from its container color down to the surface): «Сейчас играет» and ⌄ on top, the cover across the width (it sinks back a little while paused), title, artist · album, the seek bar with the time played and the time left, previous / play / next (previous restarts a track after 3 s), «К записи», then «Далее»: the tracks to come, a tap skips to one. A recording key pauses the music; so does a video in the viewer. A sound opened in the viewer plays through the player. Android plays on in the background with the system's media notification, the lock screen and headset buttons; the browser's media keys and panel work too.

## Visual language
The identity is the "Admiral Benbow" inn (vision.md#identity): a cozy home base, warm light, parchment, ink, maps. Apply it lightly through the palette (warm neutrals, lantern amber, sea blue), paper textures, the icon, empty states and small illustrations. Avoid pirate kitsch and themed chrome that gets in the way. Material 3 + Expressive components and motion, behind our `BenbowTheme` tokens. Optional dynamic color, curated warm palettes. Bundled fonts with full Cyrillic (e.g. a UI sans like Onest/Manrope/Inter and a reading serif like Literata/Lora for journal text; final pick in the design pass). Fonts must be bundled for offline use, including on web. Note backgrounds are theme-aware color pairs + optional illustration/texture.
As built (6.5): "Ещё → Оформление" per device: Как в системе / Светлая / Тёмная, and palettes Таверна (default), Море, Пергамент, Цвета системы (Material You, Android). Palettes are complete M3 schemes generated from seed colors by MaterialKolor (HCT, like Material Theme Builder), so surfaces and containers carry the palette too. System bar icons follow the chosen mode. Note colors: an entry's menu → "Фон" (Keep's ten colors or none); the whole page and its card take the color. The name "Benbow" (the "Ещё" screen, the lock screen) is set in Meddon, a face after 18th-century English handwriting (OFL, docs/licenses/Meddon-OFL.txt); nothing else uses it. App icon (6.5): original vector art, the inn's brass lantern with a small flame and a warm halo over the night sea with a low wave; adaptive with a monochrome layer (themed icons); "Benbow Dev" shows it on slate with a stripe; the desktop window uses the same picture.

## Psychology
Evidence-informed practices exposed as **optional** templates, prompts and features. Never forced.
| Practice | Source idea | Feature |
|---|---|---|
| Expressive writing | Pennebaker: writing freely about emotions reduces stress | "Free write" mode: blank page, optional timer, private by default |
| Gratitude / Three Good Things | Seligman; Emmons & McCullough | Template + optional evening prompt |
| Affect labeling, emotional granularity | Lieberman; Feldman Barrett: naming emotions precisely regulates them | Mood check-in with an emotion-word picker (category "Emotions") |
| Cognitive reappraisal (CBT thought record) | Beck: situation → thought → feeling → evidence → balanced thought | Template |
| Self-distancing | Kross: reflect on yourself from a distance or in the third person | Retrospective comments on old entries, "write to past/future self" prompts |
| Savoring and reminiscence | Bryant | On this day, resurfaced favorites, yearly recap |
| Self-compassion | Neff | Prompts after low-mood check-ins ("what would you tell a friend?") |
| Small wins | Amabile, progress principle | Weekly review template, soft stats |
| Reflection cycles | — | Weekly/monthly review templates ("This week", "This month") |
Anti-patterns to avoid: streak loss shaming, forced daily prompts, gamified points, performance metrics, nagging notifications (reminders are opt-in, gentle, snoozable). App lock (biometric) for a sense of privacy. As built (6.4): "Ещё → Блокировка", see ADR-022; the lock screen asks at once and has "Открыть" to ask again.

## Performance budgets (validated in Phase 6 with macrobenchmarks)
Cold start to Home <600 ms on the owner's phone. Entry open <100 ms. Feed scroll: no janky frames at 120 Hz. Typing: no dropped frames on a 20k-char entry.

Measured in 6.7 (2026-10-14, S24+, `apps/benchmark` on the seeded bench build: 300 entries, 120 12-MP pictures; Android Studio mirroring was on, so absolute numbers are a little pessimistic):
- Cold start (time to first frame): 388 ms before (no R8, library profiles only) → 258 ms (R8 + the app's baseline profile). APK 44 → 8 MB.
- Feed skim (fast flings): frame overrun P90 / P95 / P99 = 2.7 / 7.1 / 16.6 ms before → 1.1 / 2.1 / 6.3 ms after parsing post text off the main thread, `contentType` and a cache window (a screen ahead, half behind). No main-thread frame over 8 ms left; by hand (adb swipes) 0.8% of frames miss the 120 Hz deadline.
- Entry open: settled in ~50 ms (3 frames of 14–22 ms: the post screen's first composition and text layout). Back to the feed costs one ~30 ms frame: Navigation3 drops the feed while a post is open, so it is composed and laid out again. Keeping it composed needs an overlay scene; postponed (one frame at the start of a fade).
- Typing: unchanged, see editor.md (fine to ~5k chars, long-form needs a chunked editor).

Measured in 7.13 N1 (2026-10-17, S24+, old and new feed built the same day; until then the seed was stopped after 10 s, partway, in every run):
- Feed skim, frame overrun P90 / P95 / P99: old 1.9 / 2.8 / 5.3-6.1 ms (300 posts at most), new 2.1 / 3.1-3.3 / 5.9-6.1 ms; a six-year journal (5,300 posts) 2.2 / 3.2 / 5.9, a far skim 1.9 / 2.7 / 4.8. The extra 0.2-0.3 ms are animation frames (the month under the title, «К сегодня»): twice as many frames run animations; moving windows recompose little (~10 ms per skim).
- Web (sql.js on this PC, 5,373 posts): the index takes 24 ms on the main thread each time the journal changes while the feed is open (a covering index would halve it; sql.js spends ~10 ms just handing over the rows); a window of 160 posts 2 ms. Opening the feed: at most 79 ms busy (the old feed of 300 posts: 123 ms), again 6-11 ms (old 51-72 ms).
